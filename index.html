New WWW 2026 CCF A
Rethinking the Hidden Risk of Reranking: Achieving Risk-aware Reranking with Information Gain for RAG with LLMs
Zhizhao Liu, Zhihua Wen, Zhiliang Tian*, Zhen Huang, Miaorong Zhu, Zimian Wei, Yifu Gao, Liang Ding, Dongsheng Li*
Accepted by The Web Conference 2026 (WWW '26), Dubai, UAE. (AI Search Track)

Core Finding: We reveal a counter-intuitive phenomenon where rerankers, while improving ground-truth recall, significantly increase the proportion of "Harmful Documents"—misleading content sharing superficial shortcuts (like high-frequency entities) with the query.

Method: We propose a risk-aware reranking method utilizing Information Gain. By balancing the potential benefit against the risk of retrieved documents (akin to hard negative mining), our approach effectively suppresses harmful noise while maintaining performance.

Scenario-independent Uncertainty Estimation for LLM-based Question Answering via Factor Analysis
Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2025 (WWW '25), Sydney, Australia.

A novel plug-and-play framework for enhanced uncertainty estimation in LLM-based QA systems. We introduced scenario-specific sampling and factor analysis models to disentangle uncertainty factors without requiring model retraining.
