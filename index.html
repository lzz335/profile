Profile

I'm Zhizhao Liu, currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science and Technology at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT). I received my Bachelor's degree from Northeastern University.

My research interests primarily lie in Natural Language Processing, with a special focus on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), text generation, and hallucination mitigation. I combine insights from my cross-disciplinary background in mechanical engineering and computational mathematics to approach problems from unique angles.

New WWW 2026 CCF A

Rethinking the Hidden Risk of Reranking: Achieving Risk-aware Reranking with Information Gain for RAG with LLMs

Zhizhao Liu, Zhihua Wen, Zhiliang Tian*, Zhen Huang, Miaorong Zhu, Zimian Wei, Yifu Gao, Liang Ding, Dongsheng Li*

Accepted by The Web Conference 2026 (WWW '26), Dubai, UAE. (AI Search Track)

Core finding. We reveal a counter-intuitive phenomenon where rerankers, while improving ground-truth recall, significantly increase the proportion of "Harmful Documents"—misleading content sharing superficial shortcuts (like high-frequency entities) with the query.

Method. We propose a risk-aware reranking method utilizing Information Gain. By balancing the potential benefit against the risk of retrieved documents (akin to hard negative mining), our approach effectively suppresses harmful noise while maintaining performance.

Scenario-independent Uncertainty Estimation for LLM-based Question Answering via Factor Analysis

Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2025 (WWW '25), Sydney, Australia.

A novel plug-and-play framework for enhanced uncertainty estimation in LLM-based QA systems. We introduced scenario-specific sampling and factor analysis models to disentangle uncertainty factors without requiring model retraining.

v1.0 Desktop App MIT

PhotoRecover 照片恢复

Android data recovery for Windows · designed and built independently · 2026

A desktop utility that brings back accidentally deleted photos and files from Android phones without root. It scans the places deleted data actually survives — hidden app caches, thumbnail stores and recycle bins — previews what it finds in a thumbnail grid, and restores the selected files back to the PC.

  • Dual-channel device access: streaming scan over ADB, plus a Windows MTP (Shell COM) fallback when USB debugging is unavailable.
  • Residual-data indexing: surfaces leftovers in .photoShare, .thumbcache and trash directories, with filters for type, size, date and source folder.
  • Workflow built for real devices: thumbnail grid with preview, JSON scan snapshots for instant reload, and a concurrent batch exporter with cancellation and duplicate skipping.
  • Shipped, not just demoed: packaged as a single Windows executable via PyInstaller, with an in-app recovery guide and a README that states the hard limits instead of overpromising.
